Meet Our Feathered Friends

From urban sparrows to magnificent parrots, birds enrich ecosystems across the planet.

Macaw Parrot

Highly intelligent and social birds found in rainforests. Known for brilliant plumage.

Hummingbird

Tiny nectar-feeders with rapid wingbeats. Vital pollinators for flowering plants.

Owl

Nocturnal hunters with exceptional eyesight and quiet flight. They keep pest populations in check.

House Sparrow

Familiar urban birds that benefit from small birdhouses, fresh water, and seed feeders.

Kingfisher

Famous for swift diving skills near clean lakes and rivers. A symbol of healthy waters.

How to Be Kind to Birds

Simple changes in our backyards and balconies can keep local wild birds safe and nourished.

💧

Provide Water Bowls

Leave clean water on balconies or yards, especially during hot summer months.

🕊️

Prevent Window Collisions

Apply window decals or stickers to prevent birds from flying into glass reflection.

🌿

Plant Native Trees

Native trees provide natural food sources like berries, seeds, and safe nesting spots.

🩺

Help Injured Birds

Contact an avian rescuer for guidance when you find an injured bird.

Bird Sanctuary Partnerships

Protecting bird migration routes and preserving forests requires community action.

Protect Nesting Sites

Avoid trimming trees during spring nesting season to keep eggs and chicks safe.

Feed Responsibly

Offer grains, seeds, and fresh fruit instead of processed bread or salty foods.

Support Refuges

Volunteering with wildlife reserves helps rescue injured migratory birds.

Spread Awareness

Teach your community about local bird species and their ecological value.
