Explore Aquatic Life

From small freshwater species to majestic ocean life, every fish plays a vital role in keeping ecosystems balanced.

Clownfish

Known for living safely among sea anemones. Highly social and vital to coral reef health.

Goldfish

Popular freshwater fish that require clean water, filtration, and large tanks to thrive.

Betta Fish

Vibrant and territorial. They need warm, gentle water and peaceful habitats rather than small bowls.

Angelfish

Graceful tropical fish that flourish in spacious aquariums with plenty of plant cover.

Wild Reef Fish

Inhabit natural coral systems. Protecting them requires keeping plastic out of the water.

How We Can Protect Water Ecosystems

Pollution and habitat destruction threaten marine species globally. Small actions make a huge difference.

🌊

Reduce Plastic Use

Single-use plastics often end up in rivers and seas, harming fish and marine life.

🧼

Mind What Goes Down Drains

Avoid flushing harmful chemicals that contaminate natural waterways.

🚫

Never Dump Pets

Never release captive fish into local ponds; they can disrupt local wild populations.

🪸

Support Coral Reefs

Reefs provide shelter and food for over 25% of all ocean species.

Responsible Fish Keeping

If you keep fish at home, ensure they live in a clean, spacious, and safe environment.

Proper Tank Size

Fish need space to swim. Bowls are often too small and lack proper oxygen levels.

Filtration & Heating

Maintain proper water filters, temperature controls, and regular water changes.

Balanced Nutrition

Feed appropriate species-specific food and avoid overfeeding to keep water clean.

Long-Term Commitment

Some fish live for many years. Ensure you can provide consistent care.
